CHAPTER 04 · NOCTIS ARC

The Bell Beneath
Noctis

In a city that has forgotten sunrise, one impossible bell begins to ring.

Mara's shadow steps away from the wall. Orin's coat drips seawater onto dry stone. Above Noctis, the crimson moon opens like an eye—and a bell rings beneath the city. Every bell here was silenced a century ago.

01 / THE SECOND SHADOW

The shadow turns toward Mara. “The keeper carries the key,” it whispers. Orin looks at it in alarm. “That voice is not yours.”

02 / THE MARKET OF BORROWED NAMES

A girl named Sable guides Mara through a market of hanging names. “The city doesn't steal memories,” she says. “It shelves them. Someone has started taking them down.”

03 / THE STAIR BELOW THE BELL

A bronze bell hangs over water that reflects a sky no longer there. It has no clapper. Mara's compass points straight down. Orin warns that the next sound may use a voice she loves.

04 / THE OTHER MARA

The bell sounds without moving. Mara hears her grandmother laugh. Behind glass stands another girl in the same blue coat. “You are the echo,” she says. “I am the memory.”

THE CLIFFHANGER

The moon
is listening.

Every shadow in Noctis turns toward the glass chamber—including Orin's. The other Mara raises her hand and mouths three words:

“The moon is listening.”
